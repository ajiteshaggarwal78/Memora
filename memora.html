Our mission

Made for the North East,
with love.

Memora began with a simple question: how do we help our parents and grandparents stay sharp, joyful, and independent as the years pass?

We build AI that listens patiently, plays gently, and notices the small changes that matter — while keeping sons, daughters, and caregivers informed, wherever in the world they are. From Jorhat to Aizawl, Memora is designed for the families of India's North Eastern Region.
